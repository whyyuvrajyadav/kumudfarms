About KUMUD Farms and its founder, Yuvraj Yadav

KUMUD Farms makes small-batch desi buffalo ghee on a family dairy farm in Lucknow. I am Yuvraj Yadav, and this page tells you how it began and who is behind every jar.

How KUMUD began

My grandfather was the first in our family to build a dairy business. The farm, with its cows, buffaloes and a pond, is still the heart of our family’s work.

I started KUMUD to carry that work into Lucknow kitchens. Ghee was the clear place to begin. We already had loyal customers who came back for it, and the milk comes straight from our own farm.

The first jars went to family friends and people I know, mostly through WhatsApp. Today every batch gets its own number, starting at KMD-001, so any jar can be traced back to the batch it came from.

We make our ghee from fresh cream, the cream-based method, and not the curd-churned bilona method. We say so openly, because you should know exactly what you are buying. Here is the difference.

Kumud is the Sanskrit word for the lotus. We chose it for that meaning.

Meet Yuvraj Yadav

Yuvraj Yadav is the founder of KUMUD Farms in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. He comes from a dairy farming family, studies BCA at the University of Lucknow, and built KUMUD to bring his family farm’s ghee straight to families in the city.

He runs KUMUD on a simple idea: you should be able to know where your ghee comes from, who made it, and how. That is why he shares the farm openly, in raw and unedited videos, on Instagram and YouTube.
