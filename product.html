How to use it

Finish dal with a spoonful, cook parathas and rotis in it, make halwa, or temper spices for a tadka. Keep the lid tightly closed in a cool, dry place and use a dry spoon. See 7 ways to use desi ghee.

Questions about our ghee

Is this bilona ghee?

No. We use the cream-based method, not the bilona curd-churning method. Read the difference in our journal.

Where does the milk come from?

From buffaloes on our own family dairy farm, so there is no middleman between the farm and your jar.

Which size should I buy?

Start with 250 ml if you are trying it for the first time. The 1 litre jar costs the least per ml. See the price comparison.
