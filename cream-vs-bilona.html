Cream-based ghee vs bilona ghee: what is the difference?

6 November 2026

Bilona ghee starts with curd. Milk is set into curd, the curd is churned into butter, and the butter is simmered into ghee. It takes more time and usually more milk, so it is often priced higher.

Cream-based ghee skips the curd. The fresh cream is taken from the milk, turned into butter and clarified gently into aromatic ghee. Learn the step-by-step process in how ghee is made from fresh buffalo cream.

Both are traditional Indian methods. We make KUMUD Pure Desi Ghee from our own buffalo milk by the cream-based method and state it openly on our label, so you always know what you are buying for your kitchen.
