How to buy pure desi ghee in Lucknow

By Yuvraj Yadav, founder of KUMUD Farms, Lucknow

Colour and aroma alone cannot tell you whether ghee is pure. These checks help more.

  • Ask where the milk comes from. A farm or seller who knows the source can tell you.
  • Ask how it is made. Cream-based and bilona are different methods, and an honest seller names which one.
  • Look for a batch number. It shows the ghee can be traced.
  • Read the label. The ingredients should show ghee and nothing else.
  • Check the seal. Choose a jar with tamper-proof packaging.
  • Compare per 100 ml. Divide the price by the size to compare fairly. Here is how.
