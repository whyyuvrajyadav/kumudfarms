How to store ghee at home so it stays fresh

6 November 2026

Ghee is stable, but it keeps best when you look after it. The storage advice on the KUMUD jar is simple: store it in a cool, dry place and keep the container tightly closed.

Use a clean, dry spoon every time. Water or crumbs from a wet spoon can spoil ghee early.

Keep the jar away from the stove, steam and direct sunlight. A kitchen cupboard works well, and shade matters more in a hot Lucknow summer.

Ghee changes with the weather. It turns firm and grainy when it is cool and soft or liquid when it is warm. A change in texture alone is not a sign that it has spoiled.

If it smells sour or off, or you see mould, do not use it.
