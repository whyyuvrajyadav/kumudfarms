7 ways to use desi ghee in everyday cooking

6 November 2026

Desi ghee fits into almost every Indian kitchen. Here are seven simple ways to use it.

  1. Dal tadka: heat a spoonful, add cumin, garlic and chilli, and pour it over hot dal.
  2. Parathas and rotis: cook parathas in it, or brush a little over hot rotis.
  3. Khichdi: stir a spoon into hot khichdi just before serving.
  4. Halwa: roast sooji or atta in ghee until it smells nutty, then add sugar and water.
  5. Rice: mix a spoon into hot steamed rice or jeera rice.
  6. Sabzi: roast whole spices in ghee before adding the vegetables.
  7. Sweets: bind besan ladoo with warm ghee.
