How to buy pure desi ghee in Lucknow

6 November 2026

Lucknow has many places to buy desi ghee, from sweet shops to supermarkets to home sellers. The jars can look alike, so the questions you ask matter.

Ask where the milk comes from and whether the seller makes the ghee or only resells it. Ask whether it is made from cream or from curd, and whether it is buffalo ghee or cow ghee. A seller who knows the product answers these easily.

Read the label. The ingredients, the quantity, the price and the name of the maker should be easy to find. If something is missing, ask why.
